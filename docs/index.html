Farmer Bob’s Solitaire

State

Publishing

A card table with roots.

Farmer Bob’s Solitaire is a handcrafted solitaire game played across four rows. Build each row by suit and rank, choose a fresh hand or the Daily Deal, and try to place all 52 cards before the deck runs dry. Drag or tap cards into place, replay favorite deals, and use Hint when you need a nudge.

  • Four-row solitaire
  • Replayable deals
  • Daily Deal

The next story starts at the lake.

Fishing With Duane is a new game concept now entering specification. The medallion sets the tone: time on the water, memorable catches, and a character-led outdoor world. The game’s rules, progression, and final format are still being defined.
